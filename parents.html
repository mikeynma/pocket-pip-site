For parents

What every Pocket Pip game promises, and where to find each game's help and privacy policy.

Every game

  • No adverts, ever.
  • No in-app purchases and nothing to buy inside. Coins are only ever earned in the game.
  • No accounts, no sign-up and no tracking.
  • Nothing is collected about you or your child. Each game is saved on the phone.
  • Works offline, so it's fine on a car journey.
  • Gentle: no timers that nag, and in most games no game over. Bump into something and you just carry on.

Sharing and problems

A few things can be shared, such as a photo or a problem report. They only ever go where you choose, using your phone's own share menu, and nothing is sent by itself. Each game's privacy policy says exactly what can leave the phone.

If something goes wrong, tap Report a problem in the game: it fills in the details for you. Then send it to us by email.

Questions parents ask

Is it on iPhone or iPad?

Not yet. The games come to Android phones and tablets first, on Google Play. iPhone and iPad versions are planned for later.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or newer. If your phone can get Google Play, it's almost certainly fine.

What age is it for?

The games are made for children from about 6 upwards, and for grown-ups who remember pocket pets. They're simple enough to read and play alone, with nothing scary or unkind.

Does it need the internet?

No. Every game works offline, so it's fine on a car journey or a plane.

What does it cost?

You pay once on Google Play and that's it. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Coins in the games are only ever earned by playing.

How do I keep my child's Pip safe?

In Pocket Pip, Settings > Backup makes a code you can keep somewhere safe and use to bring the Pip back on another phone.

What about the UK Children's Code?

The games are built to keep children's privacy high by default: no accounts, no ads, no chat or online play, and nothing collected about your child. Anything that shares or sends asks a grown-up first.
