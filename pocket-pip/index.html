Pocket Pip

Hatch a pixel pet who's a little bit bonkers. Nearly 300 surprises. No ads.

Meet Pip. He's small, he's pixelated, he lives in your pocket, and he is a little bit bonkers.

Hatch your egg, give your Pip a name and look after him as he grows from a baby into a grown-up. Feed him, play with him, clean up after him (yes, really) and tuck him in at bedtime. Look after him well and he'll be happy and well behaved. Spoil him with snacks and he'll turn cheeky. Forget about him and he'll send you a nudge!

What's inside

A pet with a mind of his own

  • Turn your phone upside down and he hangs on for dear life
  • Fiddle with the clock and he gets dizzy
  • Feed him one snack too many and he starts turning violet...
  • Let your phone's battery run low and he gets sleepy too
  • Hold your finger on him and a fortune ball appears with an answer for you
  • Leave him alone and he nods off standing up, then the screensaver comes on
  • Need a break? Send him on holiday to Grandma's
  • He hiccups, sneezes, chases his tail, tells terrible jokes and sings silly songs about cheese

Nearly 300 surprise moments

Pip never sits still. He skateboards, bakes, plays chess, rides a robot vacuum, has snowball fights, does magic tricks, goes stargazing, turns into a desk lamp, goes viral and even puts on his very own opera, spotlight and all. You never know what's next, and every moment you spot goes into your collection book.

32 mini-games

Noughts and crosses, rock paper scissors, bottle flip, bubble popping, freeze dancing, making tiny sandwiches and lots more. 11 are learning games: quick sums, counting, telling the time, patterns, picture sudoku and a secret code to crack.

Teach him tricks

18 tricks to discover, from a wave and a bow to star jumps and the moonwalk.

Something to celebrate all year

Pip has a birthday every day he's with you. And 25 festivals and special days bring their own fun: Halloween, Bonfire Night, New Year, Easter, Pancake Day, Diwali, Lunar New Year, Holi, Hanukkah and many more. Choose your country and he joins in with things from 34 countries.

A real night sky

At night his sky shows the real Moon for that night. Keep watch for shooting stars and the space station gliding over.

Earn, shop and collect

  • Earn coins by looking after Pip well
  • Spend them on 12 hats, 7 room decorations, 6 colours for his case and 9 keyrings to hang on it
  • Grow a flower in his window box
  • Try your luck on the capsule machine
  • Find him a friend: a kitten, a puppy, a chick, a robot pup or a snail
  • Earn 57 achievements and collect 179 silly titles (Sir Pip the Magnificent, anyone?)
  • Snap him in the photo booth with frames and stickers

He's got a lot to say

Around 400 sayings, jokes and fun facts, and a diary of everything he's been up to. And there are secrets hidden away for those who look...

For grown-ups

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts
  • Your Pip is saved on your phone, and nothing is collected about you
  • A grown-up answers a quick sum before anything is shared or sent
  • Up to 4 players on one phone, each with their own Pip
  • Calm mode for no flashing or shaking
  • A gentle "big adventure" setting for little ones
  • Works offline
  • Pip lets you know when he needs you, and stays quiet at night
  • Made by a dad and his son, with a lot of love (and far too many cheese jokes).
