# Fonts

Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), which allows bundling them in an app.

- **Silkscreen** by Jason Kottke: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Silkscreen
- **Fredoka** by Milena Brandão and Hafontia: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka

The files are the Latin and Latin Extended subsets from Google Fonts. `scripts/build-game.mjs` embeds them in the app so Pip's screen looks right offline (Q2).
