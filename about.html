About us

Pocket Pip Games is a dad and his son in the United Kingdom.

How it started

It started with Pocket Pip: a little pixel pet in a pocket-sized case, like the ones grown-ups remember. Dad made it, and his son played it every day and said what was fun (and what was getting a bit cringe). Pip grew from there: nearly 300 surprises, 32 mini-games and far too many cheese jokes.

Then Pip wanted games of his own. They're wrapped up and on their way, with Pip's pets in every one: peek at the presents.

How we make them

  • Small and cosy: a game you can enjoy in a few minutes.
  • Every idea gets tested by our chief tester before it goes in.
  • Original pixel art, drawn for these games.
  • No ads and nothing to buy inside, because they're made for children.
