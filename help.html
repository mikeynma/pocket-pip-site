Looking after Pip

What is the little face by the coins?

It shows how Pip feels. Tap it and he tells you why, like when he is hungry or bored.

What do the bars mean?

Food, fun, energy, clean, health and manners. Keep them up and Pip stays happy. If health gets low, Pip is in danger!

Why is Pip sad?

Something is low: look at the bars. Feed him, play with him, clean up, or put him to bed.

What does the ! mean?

Pip needs you. If you leave him too long it counts as a care mistake.

How do I feed Pip?

Tap Feed, then Meal or Snack. Snacks are fun, but too many make him chubby.

Why did Pip change colour?

He goes pink when he's embarrassed or loved, red when he's cross, green when he feels poorly, blue when he's cold, and gold when you earn a title!

How do I clean up?

Tap Clean when you see a mess. He gets washed and sparkles.

What does Scold do?

Only scold Pip when he is being naughty: he says sorry and learns manners. Scolding him for nothing makes him cross!

Pip is ill. What do I do?

Tap Medicine. Keeping him clean helps him stay well.

Why does Pip give me tips?

When you start, Pip tells you what to do the first time he needs something: feeding, playing, cleaning and more. Each tip comes once.

What happens when Pip gets old?

When Pip has been with you a long time, he becomes a wise old Pip: grey eyebrows, glasses some of the time, a walking stick, and lots of stories about the old days. Keep looking after him!

How does Pip grow up?

Baby, Kid, then Grown-up. How well you look after him decides what kind of Pip he grows into. Look after him really well and he might get a pet of his own!

What does Pip do when the game is shut?

He gets hungry and bored, so come back and look after him! Come back after an hour and a page tells you what he got up to while you were away. The longer you are gone, the more he does. The first time each day, he finds a few coins too.

Where are my pets?

Every pet you meet is kept for ever, even after a new egg. Choose who is out with Pip in Settings > Pet. Look after Pip well and you will meet more!

What do the pet colours and stars mean?

Pets come in five kinds: Common ★ (grey), Magic ★★ (blue), Rare ★★★ (gold), Epic ★★★★ (purple) and Legendary ★★★★★ (orange). The more stars, the harder they are to meet. Some only come for a special reason: the book gives you a hint.

Why did another pet come to visit?

Pets you've met drop in now and then to say hi! Choose who lives with Pip in Settings > Pet.

What is the Top 5?

When a Pip passes away, his score goes in the Top 5. Then a new egg arrives.

Bedtime

Can I turn the lava lamp off?

Yes! Shop > Room > Bedtime: tap the lava lamp to put it away, and tap it again to bring it back. It only glows when no room item is out.

How does bedtime work?

Tap Sleep to put him to bed, then Lights to turn the light off. Lights off between 7 and 9 in the evening, left off till morning, earns coins and a sleep streak.

What happens while Pip sleeps?

He dreams about his day, talks in his sleep, blows snore bubbles (tap one to pop it!) and sometimes counts sheep. Tap him to tuck him in. With the light off, look out for night visitors.

Can Pip have a bedtime story?

Sometimes when you put him to bed he asks for one. Tap Story and pick how the story goes!

What are the glow stars and the music box?

Bedtime things from the shop (Room tab). They work alongside your room item: the stars glow when the light is off, and the music box plays a little tune as he drops off.

Can Pip see the real Moon?

Yes! When the lights are off, the Moon in his night sky is tonight's real Moon, and it moves across the sky through the night. Some nights there's no Moon at all: that's a new Moon, just like the real sky, and it comes back in a few days. The Moon through his telescope is real too. On real shooting-star nights in August and December, look up in the evening.

Games and toys

Can I play hide and seek with Pip?

Sometimes Pip says Hide and seek! Close your eyes, then hides behind something. Look for his tuft or his feet poking out, and tap where he is.

How do I play Pip says?

Only do the move when Pip says "Pip says" first! If he just says "Jump!", don't tap anything. Two mistakes and the game is over.

How does the secret code game work?

Pip's message is in secret code: every letter has slid along the alphabet. Tap ◀ or ▶ to slide them all back until the word makes sense, then tap That's it! Like IBQQZ: one back is HAPPY. It turns up once you've won a few learning games.

Does Pip play old arcade games?

Yes! Look out for marching invaders, dots to chomp, a wall of bricks, barrels to jump, a giant burger and lots more. And when THE FLOOR IS LAVA, he needs a chair, fast!

What does CONTINUE? mean?

Lost a game? Sometimes a countdown starts. Tap Play before it gets to 0 to have another go at the same game straight away. It never costs coins.

Why does Pip turn into a cube?

He loves cube jumping games! Sometimes he plays a level, has lots of goes, flies through portals or builds his own level. Watch for a secret coin.

How do I play with Pip?

Tap Play, then Game for a mini-game, Toys for something to play with, or Pet to play with his pet (once he has one). Winning a game cheers him up.

Where are the toys?

Play > Toys. Make beats, play freeze dance (tap Freeze! to stop Pip mid-dance) or breathe slowly with Pip when you feel wound up. Buy the bubble popper and slime in the shop (Toys) and they go in there too.

Can I squish Pip?

Yes! Press and hold on Pip and he squishes flat like a squishy toy. Let go and he boings back.

How do I play Match the card?

Play a card with the same shape or the same number as the one on the pile. Draw gives you new cards. A +4 matches anything, and Pip hates it! Play 5 cards before Pip does to win.

How do I play Mini kitchen?

Pip orders a tiny sandwich. Tap the layers in the order he asks, bread first and last. Three wobbles and it's over. He eats it, so it counts as a snack.

How do I teach tricks?

Open the book and tap Teach. He gets it right three times, then he knows the trick for good.

What learning games are there?

Play has sums, counting, patterns, telling the time, a numbers round, picture sudoku, coding Pip to a snack, secret codes, bridge building, paper planes and a breakfast machine. They get harder as you get them right!

What is the soundboard?

Play > Toys > Board: big buttons that make silly noises, and Pip reacts to every one. There's a page just for parps. Press one too many times and he'll cover his ears!

Can two people play?

Yes! Play > Toys > 2P: put the phone down between you. Player 1 has the left button, Player 2 the right. Tug of war, a soundboard battle, or Pip says for two.

Coins, shop and book

What are keyrings?

Little charms that hang off the bottom of your case, in the shop under Cases. You can hang four at once. Some of them do things: try shaking the phone, or turning the lights off at bedtime! One is earned, not bought.

How do I get coins?

Open the daily present, look after Pip, have birthdays, sleep well, and grow flowers in the window box.

What does Jump Start do?

A fizzy drink from the shop: +50 energy, and something silly happens!

What is the shop?

Tap the bag. Buy hats, room things, toys, fidget toys, a keyring, special food, case colours, seeds and mystery capsules with the coins you earn in the game. It never uses real money.

What is the window box?

Buy a seed in the shop. It grows in the box at the top right in 8 hours, even with the app closed. Tap the flower to pick it for coins.

What is a mystery capsule?

A surprise hat or toy from the shop. Some hats only come from capsules! If you get one you already have, you get some coins back.

What is the collection book?

Tap the book. It keeps everything you have seen and earned: events, Pips raised, pets, shop things, achievements and titles. It lasts for every Pip.

Can I watch an event again?

Yes! Open the book and tap an event you have seen. Pip does it again, just for fun: it gives no coins.

What are titles?

Do things (feed 100 meals, keep a sleep streak...) to earn titles like Chef or the Tidy. Pick one for before his name and one for after in the book.

What do the stars on events mean?

In the book, every event has stars: ★ happens any time, ★★ needs the right time, weather or a pet, ★★★ only comes in one season or after something you do, ★★★★ is very rare and ★★★★★ is a real secret. Keep watching!

Where is the ice lolly?

Ice lollies are in the shop in the summer (June to August), once the Summer pack has opened. They cost 5 and make Pip happier.

What is the trophy?

It pops up when something new goes into your book.

What is the daily present?

Come back every day for a present. Days in a row make a streak, with a bigger present every 7th day.

How do I take a photo?

Tap the camera, pick a frame and a sticker, then Share.

What is Most wanted?

At the top of the pets in the book: three pets you haven't met yet, with a hint about how to meet them. They change every day.

What is the Records page?

Every song Pip sings goes in the book under Records. Tap one to hear it again!

Things Pip does

Does Pip do anything on special days?

Yes! Around Valentine's Day, Pancake Day, Easter, Halloween, Fireworks night and New Year, look out for surprises, and festivals from all around the world too. On some days he dresses up.

What are the pipes and toasters?

When nobody touches the phone for a few minutes, a screensaver comes on, like old computers had: a sheep, flying toasters, growing pipes or a bouncing box. Tap the screen to wake Pip.

What are the planes in the sky?

Sometimes in the daytime a little plane flies over pulling a banner with a message on it. Read it as it goes past! Other planes write words in the sky with smoke. Cheeky ones.

Why did Pip say Bonjour?

Once a day Pip says hello in your phone's language. He also has a snack and visitors from your part of the world. He only looks at the phone's language and time zone, and nothing is sent anywhere. Pick your country in Settings.

Why does Pip drink tea and say Hello ladies and gentlemen?

Sometimes Pip pretends to be a famous British YouTuber who loves tea and finding sneaky tricks in games. Spiffing! Once you have seen it, look in the shop.

What does Pip do in the snow and rain?

After the rain he jumps in puddles. In the snow he has snowball fights and builds snowmen. He flies kites on windy days, and more!

Why is there a chicken on the food bar?

Now and then the food bar turns into a roast chicken being eaten, just for fun. The real food bar doesn't change.

Does Pip have gadgets?

Yes! Sometimes his phone rings, he plays on a tablet, rides a robot vacuum, tries VR goggles or drives a remote control car. Watch out for autocorrect!

Why does nothing happen when I leave Pip alone?

Pip saves his adventures for when you are there to see them. After 2 minutes with no taps, a screensaver comes on and nothing new starts until you tap again.

What is the Pip-sody?

Now and then Pip puts on his very own little opera, with a spotlight. Listen out for the big finish!

What are the old internet things?

Pip loves the internet of long ago: pop-ups, dial-up, dancing babies, hamsters and home pages "under construction". Ask a grown-up about them! Some you can join in with, like picking a hat colour.

What weather is there?

Rain, drizzle, storms, fog, hail, snow, wind, sunshine, sun showers, frosty mornings and heatwaves! Sometimes there's a raindrop race: tap the drop you think will win.

Why does Pip say different things?

Pip talks like his age! A baby Pip babbles, a kid asks lots of questions (and has a grumpy teenage day), a grown-up worries about the price of bananas, and an old Pip remembers the old days.

What happens at Halloween?

In the week before Halloween Pip dresses up in a new costume every day, and spooky-silly things happen: potions, spiders, a dancing skeleton... There's apple bobbing too!

What happens on Bonfire Night?

Sparklers, a bonfire, a Catherine wheel and rockets, and some bonfire treats. If Pip has a pet, he keeps it safe from the bangs.

Why is there a ♪ by some messages?

That's Pip singing! He loves a silly song about cheese or fish.

What do the little marks on messages mean?

★ is news, ! means something is happening, a speech bubble is a fun fact or quote. Plain words are Pip talking.

What are packs?

Every two weeks a new pack opens all by itself, with new things for Pip to do and new pets to meet. Look in the book to see when the next one comes!

What happens if I shake or turn the phone?

Shake it and Pip gets dizzy. Turn it upside down and he holds on tight!

Why does Pip yawn about my phone?

When your phone battery is low, Pip reminds you to charge it.

Who is Pedro?

A little pony who never seems happy, whatever happens. Maybe one day...

Does Pip sing?

Yes! Sometimes he puts on a show with his very own songs from PIPSQUEAK RECORDS.

Are there secrets?

Lots! Some names are secret... try your favourite game or hero. Keep watching: all sorts of things happen when you least expect it.

Settings and grown-ups

Why does it ask a sum before sharing?

Sharing a photo or a backup, and sending a line, leave the game. A grown-up answers the sum first. They only need to do it once every 5 minutes.

Something went wrong. How do I tell you?

Settings > Report a problem. A grown-up answers a sum, then you can share a short note with what Pip was doing. Write what went wrong at the end.

Can my brother or sister have their own Pip?

Yes! Settings > Players > type their name and tap Add. Each player has their own Pip, coins and book. When the game opens it asks who's playing. A grown-up can remove a player (not the one playing now).

Who made Pocket Pip?

Mikeynma and his son! See Settings > About for the version number and thank-yous.

What is Big adventure?

A setting for little ones. When it's on, a Pip who can't carry on goes off on a big adventure and sends a postcard, instead of passing away. The score and the Top 5 stay the same.

What is Calm mode?

Settings > Calm mode turns off flashing lights, shaking screens and nudges. It also turns on by itself if your phone is set to reduce motion.

What is the diary?

Settings > Diary. Pip writes a few words about each day.

What is Grandma's?

Settings > Grandma's. Pip goes to stay with Grandma while you are busy, and his needs stand still. Tap Bring home when you are back.

Can I play with the sound off?

Yes! Settings > Captions shows a little word for every sound, like [MUNCH] or [BOOM], so you never miss a joke.

What are Grown-up jokes?

A setting for parents, behind the grown-up sum: Pip says a few extra lines about tired-grown-up life. Still clean, just only funny if you're 40.

Can I hide the pet or the teddy?

Settings > Dismiss pet sends it off for a nap (Recall brings it back). Settings > Put away teddy (or ball) puts it away, and Get out brings it back. They also step out of the way during events, and the pet goes to bed when Pip does.

How do I keep my Pip safe?

Settings > Backup makes a code you can keep. Settings also has sounds, music and buzz on or off.

How do I start again from scratch?

Settings > Backup > Start again from scratch. A grown-up does a sum, then types RESET. It deletes every player's Pip, book and everything else on this phone, so make a backup code first if you might want them back.

Is Pip saved if I lose my phone?

If the phone's Google backup is on, Android saves the game about once a day. Install Pocket Pip on the new phone and your Pip comes back. For extra safety, Settings > Backup makes a code you can keep.
