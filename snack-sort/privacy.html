Pip's Snack Sort: privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: Snack Sort keeps everything on your phone. It has no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no internet features, and it doesn't collect anything about you or your child. Nothing leaves the phone.

Who we are

Pip's Snack Sort is a small puzzle game made by Mikey Law and his son in the United Kingdom, the makers of Pocket Pip. If you have any question about this policy, email pocketpipgame@gmail.com.

What stays on the phone

Your progress is saved on the phone only: your level, your pets and the names you give them, the snack book and your settings. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

The game makes the phone buzz a little when you play (vibration). That happens on the phone and nothing is sent anywhere.

What leaves the phone

Nothing. The game has no sharing, sending, forms, chat or online play.

Children

Snack Sort is made for children. We don't ask for a child's name, age, email, location or photos, and we don't show adverts. Pet names stay on the phone, and rude names are refused.

Services from other companies

  • Google Play delivers the app and its updates. Google's own privacy policy covers that.
  • Android backup: if backup is switched on in the phone's settings, Android may save the game to the phone owner's own Google account, so progress isn't lost with a phone. This is Android's feature, run by Google; we can't see these backups.

Your choices and rights

We hold nothing about you, but you can always email pocketpipgame@gmail.com with a question. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
