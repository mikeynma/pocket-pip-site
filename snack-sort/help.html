Pip's Snack Sort: help

Sort the snacks so every jar holds just one kind, and Pip gets to eat!

How do I play?

Tap a jar to pick up its top snack, then tap another jar to drop it in. A snack can only go into an empty jar, or onto the same snack. If the top few snacks match, they all move together.

I'm stuck!

  • UNDO takes a move back, as often as you like.
  • AGAIN starts the level again.
  • +JAR gives you an extra empty jar for this level. You earn one every 3 levels.
  • Got a pet? Tap it for a hint, once a level.

Every level can be finished: the game checks each one before you get it.

How do I get pets?

A new pet comes to live with Pip every 5 levels. You can name it when it arrives. Open MENU, then Pets, to choose who sits with Pip or rename one. Every 10th level, you put the pets to bed instead of sorting snacks!

What is the snack book?

MENU, then Snack book, shows every snack you've found and how many jars of it you've filled. New snacks turn up as you go further: Common, Magic, Rare and Epic ones. And maybe something even rarer...

Is there a puzzle every day?

Yes: MENU, then Today's snack puzzle. A new one comes every day.

Does it cost anything to play?

No. You pay once for the game, and that's it: no ads, nothing to buy inside it.
